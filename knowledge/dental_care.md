## Brushing teeth
Daily brushing with pet toothpaste is the best home dental care. Never use human toothpaste, which can contain ingredients unsafe for pets. Start slowly and reward calm behavior. Even a few times a week helps.
Suggest-task: grooming | Brush {pet}'s teeth | 5 | teeth
Tip: Brush teeth with pet toothpaste, daily if possible.

## Signs of dental trouble
Bad breath, red or bleeding gums, drooling, dropping food, or pawing at the mouth can mean dental disease. Small breeds and senior pets are at higher risk.
Tip: Bad breath, drooling, or red gums mean it is time for a vet dental exam.

## Professional cleanings
The vet may recommend a cleaning under anesthesia. Seniors need a health check first. Ask what pre-anesthetic testing is advised.
Tip: Ask the vet about a dental exam at each checkup.
