## Emergency signs
Call a vet or emergency clinic right away for trouble breathing, collapse, seizures, repeated vomiting, a swollen hard belly, bloody vomit or stool, inability to urinate, suspected poisoning, or severe pain. These cannot wait for an app.
Tip: For emergency signs, contact a vet immediately instead of following a schedule.

## Call the same day or next day
Appetite loss, lethargy, limping, a new cough, increased thirst, or changes in urination or stool should be mentioned to the vet soon.
Tip: Report ongoing changes in appetite, thirst, energy, or stool to the vet.

## Routine care
Annual or six-monthly checkups, vaccines, parasite prevention, and dental exams keep problems small.
Tip: Keep routine vet visits on the calendar.
