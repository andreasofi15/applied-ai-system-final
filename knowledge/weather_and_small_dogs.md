## Cold weather
Small and short-haired dogs lose heat quickly and seniors feel cold more. Shorten outdoor time in cold weather, use a coat if needed, and dry paws after walks.
Max-minutes: walk=15
Tip: Shorten walks in the cold and dry paws afterward.

## Hot weather
Walk in the cooler morning or evening, avoid hot pavement, bring water, and never leave a dog in a parked car. Short-nosed, older, and overweight dogs overheat more easily.
Tip: Walk during cooler hours in hot weather and carry water.

## Rain and slippery ground
Wet or icy ground is a fall risk for senior dogs with stiff joints.
Tip: Choose a shorter, safer route when the ground is wet or icy.
