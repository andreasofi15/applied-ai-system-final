## Cat feeding
Adult cats usually eat two or more measured meals a day. Wet food adds water, and fresh water should always be available. Sudden loss of appetite in a cat, especially for more than a day, needs a vet call.
Tip: Feed cats measured meals on a schedule and call the vet if a cat stops eating.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and keep one box per cat plus one extra. Changes in urination, straining, or blood need prompt vet attention.

Tip: Scoop the litter box daily and watch for straining.

## Play and enrichment
Cats need daily play, climbing spots, and scratching posts. Wand toys for ten to fifteen minutes a day help indoor cats stay fit.
Suggest-task: enrichment | Play session with {pet} | 10 | play
Tip: Give indoor cats a daily play session and scratching options.

## Senior cat care
Senior cats should have vet checkups about every six months, with attention to weight, kidney function, thyroid, and teeth.
Tip: Weigh senior cats regularly and schedule six-monthly vet checkups.
