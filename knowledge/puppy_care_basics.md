## Vaccines and vet visits
Puppies need a series of vaccinations and checkups in their first months, then deworming and parasite prevention. Follow the vet's schedule.

Tip: Follow the vet's puppy vaccine schedule.

## Potty training and routine
Take puppies out after meals, naps, and play, and first thing in the morning. Praise right after they go outside.
Tip: Take puppies outside after meals, naps, and play.

## Sleep and socialization
Puppies sleep a lot. Safe, gentle exposure to people, sounds, and other vaccinated dogs builds confidence.
Tip: Let puppies nap and introduce new things gently.
