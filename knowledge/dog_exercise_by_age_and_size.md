## Small adult dogs walks
Small adult dogs usually do well with two or three walks a day, about 20 to 30 minutes each, plus play. Needs vary by energy level.
Tip: Give small adult dogs two or three walks a day with some play.

## Senior small dog walks
Senior small dogs do better with short, gentle walks, around 10 to 20 minutes, a few times a day. Let them sniff and set the pace. Cut the walk short if they pant heavily, lag behind, or limp.
Max-minutes: walk=20
Tip: Keep senior small-dog walks short, about 10-20 minutes, at their own pace.

## Puppy exercise
Puppies need short play sessions and brief walks, because growing joints are easy to overdo. A common rule of thumb is about five minutes of structured exercise per month of age, once or twice a day, with extra free play.
Max-minutes: walk=20
Tip: Keep puppy walks short and let growing joints rest.

## Large dog exercise
Large and high-energy dogs often need an hour or more of activity a day, including running or fetch. Avoid intense exercise right after eating.
Tip: Give large active dogs an hour or more of daily exercise, but not right after meals.
