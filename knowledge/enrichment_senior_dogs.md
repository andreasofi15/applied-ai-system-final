## Mental stimulation
Sniffing games, snuffle mats, food puzzles, and gentle training keep an older dog's mind active without stressing the body. Ten minutes a day helps.
Suggest-task: enrichment | Sniff game or puzzle feeder for {pet} | 10 | sniff
Tip: Add a short sniff game or puzzle feeder each day for mental enrichment.

## Gentle play
Choose soft toys and short play sessions. Avoid jumping and sudden twisting for dogs with sore joints.
Tip: Keep play gentle and low-impact for older dogs.

## Company and rest
Senior dogs often want more companionship and quiet rest. Give them a warm, quiet bed away from drafts.
Tip: Give seniors a quiet, warm resting spot.
