## Adult and senior dog meals
Most adult and senior dogs do well with two measured meals a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart. Consistent times help digestion and make it easy to notice appetite changes. Follow the portion guidance on the food label and adjust with your vet.
Tip: Feed adult and senior dogs two measured meals a day at consistent times.

## Small breed feeding
Small dogs have fast metabolisms and small stomachs, and some are prone to low blood sugar, so long gaps between meals can be a problem. Avoid free-feeding if weight is an issue, and avoid rich fatty extras.
Tip: Do not leave small dogs without food for very long stretches, and keep portions measured.

## Puppy feeding
Puppies usually eat three or four small meals a day until about six months, then move to two. Use food made for puppies and keep times regular.
Tip: Feed puppies three or four small meals a day on a regular schedule.

## Treats and diet changes
Treats should stay a small share of daily food. Change foods gradually over about a week to avoid stomach upset. Always keep fresh water available.
Tip: Mix new food in gradually over about a week and keep fresh water out.
