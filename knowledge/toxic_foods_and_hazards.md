title: Toxic foods and household hazards
species: dog, cat

## Foods dogs should never eat
Chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, macadamia nuts, and anything with xylitol (a sweetener in some peanut butters, gum, and baked goods) are dangerous for dogs. Alcohol and caffeine are also unsafe.
Tip: Keep chocolate, grapes, onions, and xylitol products away from dogs.

## Foods cats should never eat
Lilies are extremely dangerous for cats. Onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes, and raisins are also unsafe. Never give cats dog flea products.
Tip: Keep lilies and onion or garlic away from cats.

## If your pet eats something toxic
Call the vet or a pet poison helpline right away. Do not wait for signs to appear and do not induce vomiting unless a professional tells you to.
Tip: Call the vet or a poison helpline immediately after any toxic ingestion.
