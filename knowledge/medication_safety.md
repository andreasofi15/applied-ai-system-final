## Giving medication on schedule
Give prescription medication at the same times every day, exactly as the label or the vet says. If a dose is missed, call the vet or pharmacist for advice instead of doubling the next dose. This system never gives doses.
Tip: Give medication at the same times daily exactly as prescribed, and call the vet if a dose is missed.

## Food and pills
Some medications go with food and some on an empty stomach. Follow the label or ask the vet. Hiding a pill in a small treat can help, but check that the treat is safe for your pet.
Tip: Ask the vet whether a medication should be given with food.

## Never share human medicine
Many human medicines are dangerous for pets, including ibuprofen, acetaminophen, and naproxen. Never give your own medication to a pet. Call the vet or a pet poison hotline right away if a pet swallows something.
Tip: Never give a pet human medication unless the vet says to.

## Tracking and refills
Mark off each dose and request refills before the supply runs out. Do not stop a prescription early without asking the vet.
Tip: Keep a dose log and order refills a few days early.
