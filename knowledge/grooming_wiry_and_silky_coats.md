## Brushing
Yorkie and Schnauzer coats tangle and mat easily. Brush several times a week and check behind the ears, under the legs, and around the collar or harness area where mats form.
Tip: Brush coat several times a week to prevent mats.

## Professional grooming
Many Yorkie and Schnauzer type dogs need a haircut or trim every four to eight weeks. Beards and face hair collect food and need wiping after meals.
Tip: Schedule a groomer visit every four to eight weeks.

## Nails, ears and eyes
Trim nails every few weeks, because long nails change how a dog walks. Check ears for redness or smell, and wipe tear stains gently. Schnauzer type ears and beards need regular checks.
Tip: Trim nails every few weeks and check ears for redness or odor.

## Bathing
Bathe about every three to six weeks with a dog shampoo. Dry well, especially in cold weather for small dogs.
Tip: Bathe small dogs every few weeks and dry them well.
